How the estimate is made

  • Recent form: last 5–10 completed matches, when available.
  • Head-to-head: previous meetings found in the teams' schedules.
  • Scoring strength: points/goals scored and conceded.
  • Context: home advantage and data coverage, where available.
The weighting is a transparent baseline model, not a trained betting model. It does not know confirmed line-ups, late injuries or all competition-specific factors.
MatchWise is an informational analysis tool, not betting advice. Data is supplied by ESPN's publicly accessible endpoints; availability and terms may change.
